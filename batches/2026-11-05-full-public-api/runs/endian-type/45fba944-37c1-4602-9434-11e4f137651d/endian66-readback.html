Overview

For endian-type 0.2.0, six generic BigEndian/ LittleEndian bitwise methods preserve the underlying operator result. Sixty integer From method bodies additionally implement exact identity or byte reversal under explicitly supplied x86_64 little-endian scalar-operation models. LittleEndian construction and conversion to an integer preserve the stored word; BigEndian construction and conversion to an integer reverse its bytes; cross-wrapper conversion reverses the stored word. These contracts cover every bit pattern of u16/u32/u64/u128/usize and i16/i32/i64/i128/isize. Pointer-sized Lean theorems explicitly require System.Platform.numBits=64. Original source and Cargo.toml are unchanged. Fifty executable external-operation models compose official byte primitives and are proved against independent bitvector specifications; Kani separately checks native Rust operations against explicit reversed-byte references for all inputs. Separate ordinary Kani harnesses directly check forty concrete integer byte APIs (ten harnesses, 220 checks) and sixty integer instantiations of six slice methods (1030 checks, including 180 specification assertions and 20 unsupported-path guards). The slice checks are limited to initialized, correctly aligned exact-length repr(C) leading-field buffers with arbitrary contents and replacement values; they do not establish arbitrary-T, arbitrary allocation-context, invalid/short/misaligned-buffer, or Rust aliasing guarantees. These supplemental Kani checks are not among the 66 Aeneas machine claims.
