## Differences and limits

The only archived probe member that differs from the current probe tree is
`README.md`. Its post-capture edit adds the canonical archive hash/count and
audit status; source, checker, generated proof inputs, native MIR, and proof
outputs are unchanged. The audit files themselves are post-capture sidecars
and are not members of the immutable tarball.

The checker’s Cargo-record audit is location-sensitive. For replay I pointed
`CARGO_TARGET_DIR` at the original captured directory and verified its four
files against the archived hashes; I did not rebuild it. The scripts also
refer to `/workspace/bytes-proof-tools`, and the binary payloads are external,
so reproducing in a moved environment needs the documented tool installation
and path setup.

This gate covers the closed nonempty Box first-promotion path, lexical child
automatic Drop, root read, return evaluation, and normal root Drop. It is not
admission of the complete bytes crate. CAS-loser behavior, concurrency,
unwind, arbitrary move/Drop equivalence, compiler/MIR adequacy, allocator and
pointer-provenance soundness remain outside the proof/checker claim.
