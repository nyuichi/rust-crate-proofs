# Comparison semantics and limits

The generated cross-type `PartialEq` bodies reduce to the generated byte-slice
equality model. The proofs establish that successful equality results are true
exactly when the resulting `Slice U8` values are equal, including
Bytes-vs-BytesMut symmetry.

The generated standard-library `Slice.Insts.CoreCmpPartialOrdSlice.partial_cmp`
is an axiom in this extraction. The extension therefore proves delegation to
that function, but does not claim to prove its Rust implementation. The
`byteSlicePartialOrdLexLaw` hypothesis records the additional semantic law
needed to interpret that opaque call as lexicographic order. The local
`u8ByteListLexOrder` implementation is proved equal to Lean's pure
`List.compareLex`, and Aeneas' scalar `u8` partial-order model is proved to
compare byte values. The law relating the opaque standard-library slice
function to this comparator remains an explicit premise.

The mapped theorem claims and their assumptions are listed in
`manifest.json`; `receipt.json` records the resulting kernel check and axiom
audit.
