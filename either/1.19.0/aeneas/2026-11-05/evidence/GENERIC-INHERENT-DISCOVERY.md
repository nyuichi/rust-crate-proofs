Exact-path lookup, public visibility, exactly-one API mapping, Lean theorem checks and transitive axiom policy remain unchanged.

Focused regression coverage checks generic enum/struct methods, rejection of an ambiguous selected specialization, and exclusion of only unrequested ambiguity.
